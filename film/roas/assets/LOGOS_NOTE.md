# Logos used in "The Ratio"

- **Social and ad platforms, Shopify and Salla:** these come from simple-icons (CC0), stored as path data in `platform-icons.json`. The paths are unaltered. Each one is placed on a tile in its brand colour.
- **Zid:** this is the official white logo from zid.sa (`/media/branding/logo-en.svg`). Only its embedded C2PA metadata was removed; the logo itself is unaltered.
- **Purpose:** the logos only name the platforms the agency works on. They do not suggest any partnership, endorsement or certification.
- **Before publishing:** check each platform's brand guidelines. Partner badges such as "Meta Business Partner" or "Shopify Partner" must not be implied unless the agency actually holds them.
