# Infinite Tamm v2 (45 s, 9:16 seamless loop)

v2 is faster and busier than v1:

- 10 nested worlds instead of 4.
- A punch zoom-out every 2 bars at 128 BPM.
- Something happens in every world.
- New music and new voices.

```bash
bash film/infinite-tamm/render2_parallel.sh
```

```bash
python3 film/infinite-tamm/mix2.py
```

The first command renders the picture and the score on 4 workers, in about 10 minutes. The second mixes the voiceover and masters the sound.

Output: `handover/launch-film/infinite/SayTamm_InfiniteTamm_v2_45s_9x16_LOOP.mp4` (not in git).

## The loop

Each world sits inside the next. The camera drifts for 60% of each step, then punches out and lands on a downbeat with an impact and a flash.

## Sound

- **Score:** 128 BPM, 24 bars, seamless at the loop point. It is synthesised in Web Audio:
  - four-on-the-floor kick, claps
  - darbuka doum/tak with rolls
  - sawtooth bass
  - a Hijaz (E Phrygian dominant) pluck hook
  - risers, an impact and whoosh on every landing
  - Hijaz pads under the intro and outro
- **Voices:** ElevenLabs eleven_v4, in `handover/launch-film/infinite/vo2/`:
  - Mansour (Saudi), Mawardy (Egyptian), Jeffery (English).
  - The closing «قول. تم.» reuses Mansour's «قول… تم!» take, because the free queue stayed busy.
- **Mix:**
  - Each line starts on its world's downbeat; leading silence is trimmed.
  - VO chain: high-pass, mud cut, de-esser, 3:1 compression, presence.
  - The music ducks under the voice by sidechain (4:1).
  - Two-pass loudnorm to −14 LUFS, with true peak set to −1.6 so the AAC file stays under −1 dBTP.

## Rules kept

- **Logo:** the original symbol SVG is used unchanged. «SayTamm» is set in Satoshi because no wordmark exists yet (OQ-05).
- **Content:** no people and no AI faces. No clients, numbers or claims.
- **Purple:** used as the accent only.
- **Map:** the map is abstract (dots and arcs) and shows no borders. It does not present SayTamm as a "Saudi agency".
