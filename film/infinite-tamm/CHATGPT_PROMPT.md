# Infinite Tamm: brief and storyboard prompt for ChatGPT

Copy everything inside the block below into ChatGPT.

```text
You are the art director and storyboard artist for a 45-second vertical (9:16) launch film for SayTamm, a digital marketing & performance agency. Build the storyboard and the visual keyframes with me.

THE IDEA: "Infinite Tamm" — one continuous camera move that zooms OUT forever. Every time something gets "done", we discover it was a tiny detail inside a bigger world. At the end, the whole world turns out to live inside the dot of the Arabic word «تم» ("done"), and the camera continues into the starting point. The film loops seamlessly.
Core message: "Tamm is not an ending. It is where the next thing starts."
The name: Saudis say «قول تم» when they want something to happen; Egyptians say «اعتبره حصل»; the English say "Consider it done". Same meaning, three cities.

THE ZOOM-OUT CHAIN (each frame contains the previous one, small and exactly at the CENTRE of the frame):
F1 — A single Electric Purple dot fills the frame (flat, crisp, no glow).
F2 — Pull back: the dot is a purple "Publish" button on a phone screen. The phone lies on a table (no hands, no people).
F3 — Pull back: the phone is on a café table in Cairo at golden hour; through the window, a Cairo street.
F4 — Pull back: that café is one lit window in a Cairo building facade at dusk; the pyramids far on the horizon.
F5 — Pull back: that facade is the image playing on a giant digital billboard in a Riyadh street at night; Kingdom Centre in the background.
F6 — Pull back: that Riyadh street is a photo on a tablet on a desk in a London studio; Big Ben through the window, rain.
F7 — Pull back: the London studio is seen inside a purple dot — the dot of a huge Arabic letter on a dark background.
F8 — Pull back: the dot belongs to the big word «تم» (leave the letters EMPTY as a white shape area; we will set the real typography ourselves). Final card: dark background, space reserved for the logo.
LOOP — the camera then pushes into the other dot, which becomes F1 again.

FOR EACH KEYFRAME, GIVE ME:
1. A 9:16 image, 1080×1920 composition.
2. The exact position and size of the NEXT world inside it (a small rectangle at the centre, about 1/6 of the frame width), so the zoom can be animated seamlessly.
3. 2–3 lines of description: what we see, the light, the motion, the sound.

THEN GIVE ME A STORYBOARD TABLE for 45 s: time, frame, on-screen action, sound, voice line. Voice lines (do not change their wording):
- In Riyadh: Saudi voice «قول تم.»
- In Cairo: Egyptian voice «اعتبره حصل.»
- In London: English voice "Consider it done."
- On «تم»: «تم.» then the end card "Say it. Tamm it." / «قول. تم.»
Sound: one continuously rising Shepard tone through the whole zoom, plus a new sound layer per world (café, city at night, rain, a soft "publish" click).

VISUAL STYLE (keep it identical in every frame):
- Premium flat illustration with depth: clean shapes, soft gradients, cinematic lighting, subtle grain. Editorial, not cartoon, not 3D plastic.
- Palette: Obsidian #09090D, Graphite #17171E, Soft White #F5F3FA, Muted #96939F, Electric Purple #6C35FF as the accent ONLY (≤10% of the frame: the dot, the button, a light, a sign). Warm natural light in Cairo, cool neon-free night in Riyadh, grey rain in London.
- Real landmarks drawn respectfully and accurately. No tourism clichés (no camels, no costumes).

STRICT RULES:
- NO people, faces or hands anywhere. Spaces and objects only.
- NO text inside the images (no Arabic, no English, no fake signage). Leave screens and billboards with abstract shapes only; we add all type ourselves.
- NO logos: do not draw the SayTamm logo or any brand, platform or client logo. Leave an empty area for our logo on the end card.
- No glow, neon, lens flares or particles. No fake dashboards, numbers or awards.
- Do not describe SayTamm as a "Saudi agency"; it works across Saudi Arabia, Egypt, the Gulf and the region.

Start with F1–F3 so I can approve the style, then continue.
