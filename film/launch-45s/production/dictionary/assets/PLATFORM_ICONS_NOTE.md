# Platform icons

`platform-icons.json` holds icon paths from [simple-icons](https://simpleicons.org) v16.38.1 (CC0 for the path data).

The marks themselves are trademarks of their owners: Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Threads and Meta (Meta); TikTok; Snapchat; X; YouTube and Google (Google).

How they are used in the film:

- Monochrome and unaltered.
- Small.
- Only to represent the channels SayTamm works on.
- Never in a way that implies partnership or endorsement.

Before any paid or public use, check each platform's current brand guidelines. LinkedIn is not included: simple-icons removed it at LinkedIn's request.
