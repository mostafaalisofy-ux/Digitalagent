# SayTamm launch film — creative rebuild prompt for Claude

انسخ النص التالي كاملًا إلى Claude داخل مشروع الفيديو الحالي.

```text
We need a CREATIVE RE-DIRECTION for the existing SayTamm 45-second 9:16 animatic.

Important: do NOT throw away the project, timing, message, logo end card, or the three-language narrative structure. Preserve the 45-second duration and the core story:

“Tamm is not an ending. It is the start button.”

The current version is technically clean but creatively dead. It reads as a wireframe explainer: a black screen, thin UI outlines, a white vertical pill, generic cards, a small purple dot, then typography. It illustrates a storyboard instead of making us feel the moment a decision becomes real.

Your mission is to transform it into a cinematic, tactile, editorial launch film with physical energy and authored visual transitions—WITHOUT rebuilding the project from zero.

## Keep exactly

- Total duration: 45 seconds, 9:16, 1080×1920.
- The narrative order and timings: anticipation → “قول تم” → activation → “اعتبره حصل” → “Consider it done” → language convergence → «تم» → SayTamm end card.
- The official original SayTamm logo only. Use this source asset; never recreate, trace, mirror, recolour, or AI-generate it:
  ../SayTamm-Offline-Preview/dist/brand/SayTamm_Symbol_Signature_White-Purple.svg
- Locked end-card copy exactly:
  SayTamm
  Say it. Tamm it.
  قول. تم.
- Palette: Obsidian #09090D, Graphite #17171E, Soft White #F5F3FA, muted #96939F, Electric Purple #6C35FF as a controlled accent (about 10% or less of the frame).
- Correct connected Arabic RTL typography. Use IBM Plex Sans Arabic where available.

## Remove or fundamentally replace

- The small top-of-screen technical annotations / timestamp-style instructions. They make the film look like an unfinished test.
- The repeated thin-outline UI cards and generic wireframe screens.
- The tall white vertical pill as the main visual device.
- Static text floating in empty black space.
- Any default glow, generic neon, particle field, dashboard, template transition, or stock-agency aesthetic.

## The new visual idea: “A decision travels through matter”

Make the purple pulse a physical, authored force—not a dot moving between interface cards. It should travel via light, reflection, touch, paper, glass, ink, a phone surface, or a practical LED line. Every time it moves, it should COMPLETE a real action.

The film must feel like one continuous chain reaction: a line or reflection exits one material and enters the next shot through a match cut. The viewer should feel that “Tamm” has weight.

Use only one of these source strategies:
1. Real macro footage / locally shot abstract material, OR
2. Graphic-first motion built from recorded paper, glass, light, typography, reflections, and original shape animation.

Do NOT use AI-generated people, AI-generated offices, AI-generated Arabic text, fake client work, or client logos.

## Scene-by-scene creative direction

### 00:00–00:04 — Before the decision
Not a blank black cursor screen. Create a tense tactile close-up: a faint cursor reflected in black glass, a hovering finger, a sheet of paper with a single unfinished stroke, or a practical light almost switched on. Let the frame breathe, but make it feel suspended—not empty.

Sound: close foley, a subtle inhale, distant electrical tone. No music yet.

### 00:04–00:08 — «قول تم»
Show a real pre-action moment, tightly framed. A finger hovers, then taps; a light is about to engage; a physical composition is about to lock into place. At the final consonant of «تم», the purple energy is released as a razor-thin reflected edge—not a round dot.

Camera: macro, shallow depth, restrained motion. The action must be tangible.

### 00:08–00:15 — First chain reaction
Turn the tap into a choreography of material transitions:

- Finger tap on black glass → match cut to a light line sliding under frosted glass.
- The line exits as a reflection on paper → the paper’s typography/geometry locks into place.
- That line wakes a dark display/space from the edge inward.

Use 3–4 precise match cuts. Each transition must finish an action; none should be decorative.

### 00:15–00:20 — «اعتبره حصل»
Do not cut to another UI card. Let a hand calmly close a phone, or let a product/studio composition click into its final alignment. The Egyptian phrase should land like quiet confidence, not a loud punchline.

### 00:20–00:27 — “Consider it done.”
Move from the intimate to the expansive. The exact purple blade/diagonal language can travel through a material or reflection, turning abstract fragments into an ordered, premium visual system. It should feel global and intentional—not like a tech UI.

### 00:27–00:35 — Three languages converge
The words `قول`, `اعتبره`, and `Consider` should exist in different physical planes—not simply type onto a black background:

- one appears as a reflection,
- one as a sharp typographic layer over a tactile surface,
- one as a moving editorial word mark.

They are pulled toward one centre by the same force. Use depth, parallax, mask reveals, and camera movement. Keep it clean; do not clutter the 9:16 frame.

### 00:35–00:40 — «تم» is the hero moment
This is the visual climax. «تم» must arrive with authority, scale, and materiality. Build it from the convergence of the three prior words; it cannot just pop onto black.

The two dots become two controlled purple beats. They guide the eye into the official SayTamm symbol reveal, but the logo itself must remain the original SVG asset—do not draw it from scratch.

### 00:40–00:45 — End card
Keep this elegant and almost still. The existing logo lockup is the one portion that can stay minimal. Improve only spacing, optical balance, and the quiet after the climax. No extra CTA, website, claims, or unnecessary copy.

## Motion and cinematography rules

- Every scene needs at least two layers of depth: foreground material + a secondary light/reflection/type layer. Avoid flat compositions.
- Begin locked-in and close; gradually widen the perceived space after the first “Tamm.”
- Use deliberate camera language: macro push, rack focus, controlled pan, parallax. No random zooms or whip pans.
- Use match cuts driven by shape, light direction, hand position, or the purple accent line.
- The pacing should accelerate through 00:35 and release into the end card.
- Purple is a signature pulse, never a permanent glow.

## Sound direction

The supplied animatic is silent; the rebuilt version must be designed with sound from the start.

- Build rhythm from tactile foley: fingertip on glass, soft click, paper friction, practical electrical hum, breath.
- The final sound of the Arabic letter «م» should evolve into a warm, low bass motif.
- Add a sparse editorial electronic score only after the first activation. No trailer booms, no generic corporate music, no overproduced risers.
- The three phrases need authentic Saudi, Egyptian, and English performances. If authentic performers are unavailable, keep the film silent except for foley and display the phrases as typography; never imitate dialects with a synthetic or non-native performance.

## First required deliverable — do this before final rendering

Do not jump directly to a new 45-second export. First revise the existing project and deliver:

1. Six labelled visual keyframes, F01–F06, representing the opening tension, first activation, chain reaction, language convergence, «تم» climax, and end card.
2. A contact sheet containing all six frames.
3. A revised shot-by-shot change log that maps every new creative decision to the existing timing.
4. A 10–15 second proof-of-energy preview covering the 00:04–00:15 activation section, with provisional foley.

Then stop and ask for review. Do not alter the master end-card copy and do not publish or upload anything.

## Definition of success

When watched with the sound off, it must look like an authored premium launch film—not an animatic, an app demo, a UI explainer, or a template. When watched with sound on, the viewer must physically feel the transition from “waiting” to “happening.”
```
