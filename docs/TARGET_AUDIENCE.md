# Target Audience

## Approved audience [APPROVED — Bible slide 6]

**Decision-makers who value clarity, speed and measurable growth.**
- CMOs and Marketing Directors
- Founders and business owners
- E-commerce and growth teams
- Regional brands entering or scaling in Saudi Arabia
- Organisations that need media, performance and strategy connected

Extended by the brief [SOURCE]: CEOs/MDs, e-commerce managers, real estate developers, retail & FMCG, enterprise and mid-market companies.

## Buying committee [RECOMMENDATION]

An agency decision in a Saudi mid-market or enterprise company usually involves several people. The website must answer each one's question within one or two clicks.

| Role | Their question | What convinces them | Where the site answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CEO / Founder / MD** (economic buyer) | "Will this move revenue, and can I trust them?" | Outcomes in business terms, senior people, clarity | Home proof section, About (leadership), case study results |
| **CMO / Marketing Director** (champion) | "Will they make me look good, and will they understand the brief fast?" | Strategic thinking, process, reporting quality | Approach, Services, sample report structure |
| **Growth / E-commerce Manager** (user) | "Do they know the platforms, the tracking and the testing?" | Technical depth, tools, testing discipline | Performance and Data & Growth service pages |
| **Procurement / Finance** (gatekeeper) | "Is this a legitimate, compliant vendor?" | Legal entity, CR number, VAT, contracts, data protection | Footer legal details, Privacy page, About |

## Personas [ASSUMPTION — to validate in 3–5 owner or client interviews]

**1. The scaling founder.** Owns a Saudi D2C or e-commerce brand that has outgrown freelancers. Wants growth they can see weekly. Decides fast, but does not trust inflated promises.
*Hook:* "Say the target. Tamm the result."

**2. The accountable CMO.** Mid-to-large company with an existing agency they are not happy with. Under pressure to show marketing's contribution to revenue. Needs a partner who can present to the CEO.
*Hook:* "Marketing without direction is just activity."

**3. The developer's marketing head.** A real estate developer launching projects, with heavy lead-gen needs and lead-quality problems. Cares about cost per qualified lead and sales-team feedback loops.
*Hook:* lead quality over lead volume. Needs a real estate case study to land.

**4. The regional entrant.** A GCC or international brand entering or scaling in KSA. Needs local market fluency, Arabic-first creative, and a partner who knows Saudi platforms and behaviour.
*Hook:* "Saudi in attitude, global in execution."

## Saudi market considerations [RECOMMENDATION]

- **Bilingual by default.** Arabic must read as native, not translated. Many senior B2B readers will browse in English, so both languages carry equal weight.
- **Mobile-first and messaging-first.** WhatsApp is a primary business contact channel in KSA. Offer it only once a number is approved (OQ-08).
- **Platform mix.** Snapchat, TikTok, X, Instagram, YouTube and Google are all commercially significant in Saudi Arabia. Service pages should reflect the platforms SayTamm actually operates, **once confirmed (OQ-15)**.
- **Trust signals.** Visible legal entity details, CR and VAT numbers, a physical presence, and senior named people carry real weight with Saudi buyers.
- **Data protection.** The Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) applies to lead forms and tracking. A consent and privacy approach is needed before launch (see `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- **Market statistics** (ad spend, penetration, growth rates) are **not included**. Any figure used must be sourced and dated before publication.

## Not the target [RECOMMENDATION]

- Buyers looking for the cheapest posting or design service.
- One-off design jobs with no performance objective.
- Prospects who want guaranteed results without shared accountability for budget and inputs.

The site should politely filter these out through its positioning, budget-range field and service framing.
