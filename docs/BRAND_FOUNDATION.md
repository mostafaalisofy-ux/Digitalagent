## The idea [APPROVED]

**SayTamm turns a request into action.**

"قول تم" is a confident Saudi response: *the request is understood, accepted, and already moving.* The identity turns that confidence into a modern digital system: decisive, measurable, execution-focused.

- **SAY** — align on the objective: the brief, the request, the idea being expressed.
- **TAMM** — execute and deliver: acceptance, completion, follow-through.

## Promise [APPROVED]

**Say it. Tamm it.** Less noise, more movement from brief to measurable outcome.

Every touchpoint should make the client feel **understood → aligned → moving**.
SayTamm is a partner that does not overcomplicate the brief.

## Values [APPROVED]

1. **Clarity** — make the next move obvious.
2. **Ownership** — take responsibility for the outcome.
3. **Speed** — reduce unnecessary friction.
4. **Precision** — optimise against evidence.
5. **Impact** — prioritise what moves the business.

## Closing principle [APPROVED]

SayTamm is not about saying yes to everything. It is about understanding the objective, making the right call, and moving with confidence.
*Strategy sets the direction. Media creates the opportunity. Performance proves the move.*

## One-line design principle [SOURCE: Full Visual Guidelines, slide 40]

**Make the next move obvious.** This is the test for every page, slide and ad.

## Brand test [APPROVED — Bible slide 44]

Before anything goes live:
- Is the logo geometry intact?
- Is the visual hierarchy clear?
- Is purple being used intentionally?
- Does the typography feel consistent?
- Does the asset communicate an outcome or an action?
- **Would the piece still feel like SayTamm without the logo?**
