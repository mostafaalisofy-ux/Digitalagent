# Business Positioning

## What is approved [APPROVED — Bible slides 3, 45]

- **Category:** Digital Marketing & Performance Agency.
- **Positioning:** a performance-led digital agency with a confident, action-oriented personality.
- **Core capabilities:** Media Buying, Performance Marketing, Strategy, Data & Growth.
- **Competitive territory:** between strategic thinking and decisive execution.
- **Promise:** less noise, more movement from brief to measurable outcome.

## What SayTamm is not [SOURCE: brief]

Not a social media management company, not a graphic design studio, not a media buying vendor, not a content production supplier.
It is a **strategic growth partner that connects marketing decisions to business outcomes.**

## Refined positioning statement [RECOMMENDATION]

> For Saudi decision-makers who are tired of marketing that produces activity instead of outcomes, **SayTamm is the performance-led growth partner that turns a brief into a plan, a plan into media, and media into measurable results.** It is built to move fast, take ownership of the number, and say clearly what happens next.

Short form (sales, bios): **Strategy, media and performance, connected. From brief to measurable outcome.**

## The gap we occupy [RECOMMENDATION]

Saudi buyers typically choose between three imperfect options:

| Option | What they get | What's missing |
|---|---|---|
| Large network / creative agencies | Big ideas, brand campaigns | Slow, layered, often weak on performance accountability |
| Media / ads vendors | Campaign setup, spend management | No strategy; optimise platform metrics, not business outcomes |
| Content / social shops | Volume of posts and creative | No link between output and revenue |

**SayTamm's territory is the connection between them.** Strategy, media and performance run as one system with one owner, reported in business terms.
Market-size and spend claims are intentionally omitted. Any statistic used publicly must be sourced and dated (see `BRAND_VOICE.md`, Claims policy).

## Differentiation pillars [RECOMMENDATION]

Each pillar is built on approved brand values. None of them depends on unverified claims.

| Pillar | What it means | Proof we will need |
|---|---|---|
| **Connected, not siloed** | Strategy, media and performance are planned and owned together | Process documentation; one case showing the full chain |
| **Outcome ownership** | We report against business KPIs (CPL, CAC, ROAS, revenue), not only platform metrics | Sample report format; KPI frameworks per client type |
| **Speed without noise** | Short decision loops, clear next actions in every report | Defined SLAs (to be agreed: OQ-14) |
| **Saudi in attitude, global in execution** | Native understanding of the Saudi market, delivered to international standards | Team credentials; Arabic-first creative examples |
| **Evidence over opinion** | Testing frameworks and data-driven optimisation | Testing log examples; measurement setups |

## Operating narrative: Say → Tamm → Prove [RECOMMENDATION]

Derived from concept board 1 ("We Say it / We Tamm it / We Prove it"). Not stated in the Bible, so it **needs approval** (OQ-12).

1. **Say** — Strategy. Planning. Clear direction. *We align on the objective and the number that matters.*
2. **Tamm** — Execution. Optimisation. Smart performance. *We launch, test and optimise against that number.*
3. **Prove** — Real results. Measurable growth. *We report what happened, why, and what happens next.*

This gives the website, proposals and reports a single repeatable structure, and it maps directly to the Bible's report rule: *"what happened, why, and what happens next?"*
