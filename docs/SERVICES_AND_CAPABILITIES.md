## Service scope [SOURCE: steps-ksa.com service pages, 2026-11-04]

### 01 — Digital Marketing Strategy
- **Includes:** foundational research (competitor positioning across KSA/GCC, target customer definition across Arabic and English behaviour); brand positioning and value proposition; messaging framework; channel strategy and go-to-market sequencing; a 90-day-plus roadmap.
- **Named deliverables:** Positioning Statement · High-Value Customer Profile · Channel Priority Plan · Budget Allocation Model · Marketing Plan.
- **Process / timing:** Discovery & Audit (wk 1) → Positioning & Customer (wk 1–2) → Strategy Blueprint (wk 2–3) → Handover & Alignment (wk 3–4). Typically 2–4 weeks.
- **Can stand alone** or hand over to the client's team.

### 02 — Branding & Identity
- **Includes:** brand strategy (market and competitor analysis, positioning, differentiation); brand messaging (naming and tagline when needed, tone-of-voice guidelines); logo and visual identity (primary logo, variations, asset library); full brand build (all of the above as one project).
- **Process / timing:** Discovery → Brand Strategy (written before any design) → Identity Design (two directions) → Feedback & Revisions → Delivery (guidelines, files, handover). Visual identity takes 3–5 weeks; a full build takes 6–10 weeks.
- **Differentiator in their copy:** Arabic and English built together from day one. *SayTamm's own identity system shows this approach in practice.*

### 03 — Performance Marketing (Paid Media)
- **Google Ads** (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube; Arabic and English intent) · **Meta** (Facebook, Instagram; city, income and behaviour segmentation; AR/EN Reels and Stories tested before scaling) · **Snapchat** (Snap, Story and Collection Ads, Lenses; 18–34) · **TikTok** (In-Feed, TopView, Branded; creative to management) · **LinkedIn** (Sponsored Content, Message Ads, Lead Gen Forms; B2B) · **Retargeting & A/B testing** (cross-platform funnels; copy, creative and landing-page tests).
- **Process:** Audit → Strategy (platform mix, budget, KPIs, Arabic creative brief) → Launch (build, tracking, creative) → Optimise (daily monitoring, weekly reports) → Scale.
- **Operating commitments published by Steps:** a revenue target before launch; direct client read access to ad accounts; weekly reports (spend, CPM, CPC, CPA, leads, ROAS vs target); free audit findings within 3 business days. **Confirm SayTamm will keep these (OQ-14).**
- This is the **Media Buying** capability named in the Bible.

## How the nine stay one system [RECOMMENDATION]

The danger of nine services is sounding like a menu. The fix is to **sell outcomes and show services as the means**:
- The homepage presents **four pillars**, not nine boxes.
- Every service page links upward to the Say → Tamm → Prove approach and sideways to its pillar partners. For example, Performance links to Analytics & Growth and to Content.
- Proposals bundle by business goal ("Launch", "Scale e-commerce", "Generate qualified leads"), not by service.
- This answers the brief's warning: SayTamm offers social, content and design, but it is not a social media company, a design studio or a content supplier, because each service is tied to a business number.
