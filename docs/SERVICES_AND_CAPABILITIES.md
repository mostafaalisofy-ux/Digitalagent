# Services & Capabilities

## Inputs

- **[APPROVED] Bible:** core capabilities are Media Buying, Performance Marketing, Strategy, Data & Growth. Four messaging pillars (Strategy, Media, Performance, Execution). Four service icons (Strategy, Media, Performance, Data).
- **[SOURCE] Brief:** five initial areas (Strategy · Media Buying · Performance Marketing · Data & Growth · Creative & Content), explicitly "not a finalized service catalog".
- **Rule:** no service, certification, partnership or capability is published until the owner confirms SayTamm actually delivers it.

## Recommended architecture [RECOMMENDATION — needs approval, OQ-13]

**Four service lines**, matching the brand system's four pillars and icons. Creative is a capability *inside* Performance and Media, not a fifth line.

Why: the brief warns against positioning as a "content production supplier". A standalone Creative & Content line pulls the brand toward that, and it breaks the four-part system the identity was built on. Creative still appears prominently, framed as **creative built to perform and be tested**.

| # | Service line | Tagline (Bible system) | What the client buys | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Strategy** | Say the objective. Tamm the plan. | Clarity on where growth comes from, and the plan to get it | Digital marketing strategy · marketing planning · market research · audience insights · brand positioning · go-to-market · growth strategy |
| 02 | **Media** | Say the audience. Tamm the reach. | Budget working where it works hardest | Paid media strategy · media planning · budget allocation · audience targeting · cross-platform execution · campaign management · platform optimisation |
| 03 | **Performance** | Say the target. Tamm the result. | A number that moves: leads, sales, ROAS | Performance strategy · lead generation · e-commerce performance · conversion optimisation (CRO) · **performance creative & creative testing** · campaign testing · ROI/ROAS optimisation |
| 04 | **Data & Growth** | (new line proposed: *Say the question. Tamm the answer.*) | Confidence in the numbers and the next decision | Tracking & measurement · conversion tracking · analytics · dashboards & performance reporting · insights · continuous optimisation |

The "Data & Growth" tagline is new. It is not in the Bible, so **it needs approval.**

### Where Creative & Content lives
- **Performance creative** (ad concepts, variants, testing frameworks) → Performance.
- **Campaign concepts** → Strategy (concept) and Media (rollout).
- **Social content** → offered only as part of a performance or campaign engagement, not as a standalone retainer. **This is a commercial decision for the owner (OQ-13).** If always-on social content is a real revenue line today, we add a fifth line called "Content", positioned around performance.

## Engagement models [RECOMMENDATION — pricing and terms MISSING]

Packaging helps buyers self-qualify and shortens sales cycles. We propose three entry points:

| Model | For | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth Partnership** | Brands wanting one accountable partner | Retainer across Strategy + Media + Performance + Data, with a monthly performance review |
| **Media & Performance Management** | Teams with strategy in-house | Retainer for paid media execution and optimisation, plus reporting |
| **Performance Audit / Sprint** | First-time clients and sceptical buyers | Fixed-scope diagnostic of media, tracking and funnel, ending in a prioritised action plan. **Strong lead-generation offer** |

Do not publish prices, timeframes or guarantees until they are approved (OQ-14).
