# AI visual prompts, batch 2 (SayTamm website)

Date: 2026-11-04. Same rules as batch 1 (`docs/AI_VISUAL_PROMPTS.md`): **illustrative and decorative only**. Never use these images for case studies, clients, the team or the office.

## How to use

1. Copy **one complete prompt** at a time (from "PROMPT START" to "PROMPT END") into ChatGPT. Don't combine two images in one request.
2. Ask for **3 variations** of each image and keep the best one.
3. If the tool can't produce the exact size, ask for the **widest landscape format available, at the highest resolution**, and keep the main subject where the prompt says. We'll crop to the final size.
4. Check every image against the list in §6 before sending it.
5. Send the files named exactly as listed. PNG is preferred (we make the WebP versions).

## 1. Work page: hero image
**File:** `work-hero.png` · **Final size:** 2400 × 1000 px (12:5, wide landscape) · **Placement:** behind the "Work that moved the number." headline at the top of `/work/`, at reduced opacity, faded at the edges. The headline sits on the left in English and on the right in Arabic, so **the subject must sit in the centre-right, and the far left and far right must be very dark.**

```
PROMPT START
Create a photorealistic, ultra-high-resolution wide landscape image (aspect ratio 12:5, 2400×1000 px) for the top of a premium digital marketing agency's "Work" page.

SUBJECT: A row of five tall, thin, matte graphite slabs (like monolithic architectural fins) standing upright on a dark polished concrete floor, evenly spaced, receding slightly in perspective from left to right. Each slab is a little taller than the one before it, so their top edges form a clean, rising 45-degree diagonal from lower-left to upper-right, a quiet visual metaphor for growth and measurable progress. The tallest, final slab on the right has one thin, razor-sharp edge lit with a soft electric purple light (#6C35FF). The purple appears ONLY on that single edge and as a faint reflection of it on the floor.

COMPOSITION: Place the group of slabs in the CENTRE-RIGHT of the frame, occupying roughly the area from 40% to 85% of the width. The LEFT 35% of the frame must be almost pure darkness (near-black #09090D) with no objects, so a large headline can sit there. The far right 10% should also fall off into darkness. The horizon/floor line sits at about 70% of the image height. Lots of negative space above the slabs.

CAMERA & LENS: Shot with a full-frame camera and a 50mm lens at eye level, slightly low (camera height about 1.2 m), f/4, very slight depth of field so the nearest slab is sharp and the far wall is soft. No dramatic distortion, no fisheye, no tilt.

LIGHTING: A single soft, cool-white key light from the upper right at about 45 degrees, grazing the slabs so their surfaces show subtle matte texture and long, soft shadows fall toward the lower left. Deep, rich shadows everywhere else. The purple edge light is a thin practical light, not a glow: crisp, controlled, no bloom, no haze.

MATERIALS & COLOUR: Matte graphite (#17171E) slabs with a very fine brushed texture; near-black obsidian background (#09090D); soft off-white highlights (#F5F3FA) only where the key light hits. Electric purple (#6C35FF) covers under 5% of the image. No other colours at all: no blue, no teal, no pink, no orange, no gradients of other hues.

MOOD: Calm, confident, architectural, expensive, editorial, like a still from a high-end architecture magazine or a luxury brand campaign. Quiet, not flashy.

STRICTLY DO NOT INCLUDE: any text, letters, numbers, words, logos, symbols, icons, watermarks or signatures; screens, devices, charts, graphs or dashboards; people, faces, hands or silhouettes; recognisable buildings, landmarks, cities or flags; neon glow, lens flare, light leaks, haze, smoke, fog, particles, sparkles, bokeh orbs, stars or dust; rainbow, multicolour or gradient backgrounds; cartoon, illustration, 3D-render, CGI-plastic or video-game look; borders, frames, vignettes drawn as lines; any thin white line along the image edges.

OUTPUT: One photorealistic image, sharp, clean, no visible noise or AI artefacts, no text anywhere. Make 3 variations.
PROMPT END
```

## 2. Final call to action: background image ("Tell us what you need")
**File:** `cta-background.png` · **Final size:** 2400 × 1000 px (12:5) · **Placement:** behind the closing "Start a project" section at the bottom of every page, at reduced opacity. The text sits on the left (English) or right (Arabic), so **the subject must sit low and to the right, and the upper two-thirds must stay dark and empty.**

```
PROMPT START
Create a photorealistic, ultra-high-resolution wide landscape image (aspect ratio 12:5, 2400×1000 px) to sit behind the final "Start a project" call-to-action of a premium digital marketing agency website.

SUBJECT: A wide, dark architectural doorway or opening in a thick matte stone wall, seen straight on. The opening is not a rectangle: its edge is cut on a single clean 45-degree diagonal rising from the lower-left to the upper-right, like a slanted architectural slot. Through the opening, a soft wash of light spills onto the floor in front of it, and along the bottom edge of the diagonal cut runs one thin line of electric purple light (#6C35FF). The metaphor: an open door, an invitation, the first step.

COMPOSITION: The opening and its light sit in the LOWER-RIGHT area of the frame (roughly from 55% to 95% of the width, and from 45% to 100% of the height). The UPPER TWO-THIRDS and the whole LEFT HALF of the frame must be deep, almost uniform darkness (#09090D to #17171E), completely empty, so a large headline and a button can sit on top. The light should fall toward the bottom edge of the image, not toward the centre.

CAMERA & LENS: Full-frame camera, 35mm lens, straight-on frontal view, camera at the height of the bottom of the opening, f/5.6, everything in focus. Perfectly level horizon, no perspective tilt.

LIGHTING: The only real light source is behind the opening, soft and warm-neutral white, spilling forward in a clean diagonal shape on the floor. The purple line is a crisp, thin practical light with no bloom. Everything else in shadow, high contrast, rich blacks without crushing all detail: the stone texture should be just visible in the shadows.

MATERIALS & COLOUR: Matte dark stone or concrete wall in graphite tones (#17171E), near-black background (#09090D), soft off-white light (#F5F3FA), electric purple (#6C35FF) under 5% of the image. No other colours.

MOOD: Inviting but serious; quiet confidence; a premium, minimal, editorial feel.

STRICTLY DO NOT INCLUDE: any text, letters, numbers, logos, symbols, icons, arrows or watermarks; people, faces, hands or silhouettes; doors with handles, signs or exit symbols; windows showing a city, sky or landscape; recognisable buildings or landmarks; neon glow, lens flare, haze, fog, smoke, particles, sparkles or bokeh; multicolour gradients; cartoon, illustration or 3D-render look; borders, frames or white lines along the image edges.

OUTPUT: One photorealistic image, clean, no AI artefacts, no text anywhere. Make 3 variations.
PROMPT END
```

## 3. Homepage "How we work": four disciplines, one connected plan
**File:** `approach-band.png` · **Final size:** 2400 × 900 px (8:3, very wide) · **Placement:** a full-width band in the "How we work" section of the homepage, above the four steps (Strategy → Creative → Media → Performance). **The four objects must sit in a row, evenly spaced across the full width, so they line up with the four columns of text below.**

```
PROMPT START
Create a photorealistic, ultra-high-resolution very wide panoramic image (aspect ratio 8:3, 2400×900 px) for the "How we work" section of a premium digital marketing agency website. The idea: four disciplines working as one connected plan.

SUBJECT: Four identical, low, square matte graphite blocks (like minimalist plinths or architectural pedestals, each about knee height) placed in a single straight row on a dark matte floor, evenly spaced, with equal gaps between them. Running continuously from the first block to the fourth, across the top surfaces and through the gaps, is ONE single, unbroken, thin line of electric purple light (#6C35FF), like an inlaid light strip, connecting all four into one system. The line rises very slightly from left to right (a gentle upward path, never downward). Each block is otherwise identical: same size, same material, same lighting, no decoration.

COMPOSITION: The four blocks are spread across the full width: block centres at roughly 12%, 37%, 63% and 88% of the image width, so they align with four equal columns. They sit in the lower half of the frame (top surfaces at about 55% of the height). The upper 45% is calm, dark, empty space. Perfectly symmetrical spacing, perfectly level floor.

CAMERA & LENS: Full-frame camera, 50mm lens, a slightly elevated three-quarter-front view (camera about 20 degrees above the block tops), f/8, everything sharp from first to last block. No fisheye, no strong perspective convergence: the four blocks should look the same size.

LIGHTING: Soft overhead light with one gentle key light from the upper left, giving each block a softly lit top face, a mid-tone front face and a soft shadow falling to the right. The purple line is a crisp inlaid light with only a very faint reflection on the floor, no bloom or glow halo. Deep shadows between blocks.

MATERIALS & COLOUR: Matte graphite blocks (#17171E) with very subtle stone or concrete texture; near-black floor and background (#09090D); off-white highlights (#F5F3FA) on the top faces; electric purple (#6C35FF) used only for the single connecting line (under 4% of the image). No other colours.

MOOD: Orderly, connected, precise, calm and premium; a visual of a well-run system.

STRICTLY DO NOT INCLUDE: any text, numbers, labels, letters, logos, icons, symbols or arrows; different shapes or sizes for the blocks; screens, devices or charts; people, hands or silhouettes; plants, furniture or props; neon glow, lens flare, haze, particles, sparkles or bokeh; multicolour gradients; cartoon, illustration, isometric-diagram or 3D-render look; borders or white lines along the edges.

OUTPUT: One photorealistic image, clean, no text anywhere. Make 3 variations.
PROMPT END
```

## 4. (Optional) Hero background loop video
**File:** `hero-loop.mp4` · **Size:** 1920 × 1080, 8 seconds, seamless loop, **no audio** · **Placement:** a very subtle, low-opacity layer behind the homepage hero. It's shown only to visitors who haven't turned on "reduced motion", and a still image is used on mobile. This needs a video model (for example Sora). Skip it if the result isn't perfectly calm: a static hero is better than a busy one.

```
PROMPT START
Create an 8-second, seamlessly looping, photorealistic video (1920×1080, 24 fps, no audio) to be used as a very subtle background behind a website hero section of a premium digital marketing agency.

SCENE: A vast, dark, minimal architectural space with a matte graphite floor and wall (#17171E) fading into near-black (#09090D). Across the wall, a single thin line of electric purple light (#6C35FF) rests on a perfectly straight 45-degree diagonal, rising from lower-left to upper-right.

MOTION: Extremely slow and calm. A soft band of white light glides very slowly along the diagonal from lower-left to upper-right, gently illuminating the wall's matte texture as it passes, then fades out; the loop restarts seamlessly with no visible jump. The camera is completely static: no zoom, no pan, no shake. Nothing else moves.

COMPOSITION: The diagonal and the moving light sit in the RIGHT HALF of the frame. The LEFT HALF is almost pure darkness for the headline. Overall the video must be very dark (average brightness low) so white text on top stays perfectly readable.

STYLE: Photorealistic architectural cinematography, crisp, minimal, premium. Colour palette strictly near-black, graphite, soft white and one electric purple accent under 5% of the frame.

STRICTLY DO NOT INCLUDE: any text, logos, numbers, symbols, people, hands, screens, particles, sparkles, dust, smoke, fog, haze, lens flares, glow bloom, flicker, strobing, fast motion, colour shifts, other colours, camera movement, cuts or transitions.

OUTPUT: One 8-second seamless loop, 1920×1080, 24 fps, no audio.
PROMPT END
```
