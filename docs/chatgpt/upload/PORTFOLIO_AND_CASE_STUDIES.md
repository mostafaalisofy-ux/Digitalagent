## 4. Red flags (non-negotiable)

1. ❌ **57357: targeting the home locations of named "Gulf princes".** The deck says donor data identified high-value donors, the team "googled Gulf princes' addresses" and geo-targeted them. **Never publish this in any form.** For a Saudi-market agency, describing location-targeting of members of Gulf royal families is a serious reputational and legal risk (PDPL, platform policies). Remove it from every version.
2. ⚠️ **Logo walls.** The Steps site shows STC, Zara, Saudi GEA (a government authority), Air Arabia, Cisco, Huawei, Costa Coffee and others. The Infinity deck shows Red Bull, Aéropostale, Bath & Body Works, Starter and others. Rights to the *material* do not include permission to imply an endorsement by those brands. Before any logo appears under SayTamm, confirm: was it a **direct client** (or via a franchisee or agency)? Who did the work? Is it allowed? Government entity logos need particular care.
3. ⚠️ **Durations** in the Infinity deck text contradict their own screenshots. Always use the dated ranges.
4. ⚠️ **Celebrity creative** (Tamer Hosny, Cheb Khalid ads for 57357): showing thumbnails as portfolio is common practice, but confirm that no talent restrictions apply.
5. ⚠️ Remove Infinity Corp and nsm+ branding from any reuse, and restyle material into the SayTamm system. Name the companies in text only, if the owner wants them named (OQ-33).
